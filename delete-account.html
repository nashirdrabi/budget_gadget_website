Account & data

Delete your account
from the app.

You can permanently delete your Budget Gadget account from the About section in Profile. Follow the path below and confirm when prompted.

Profile→About→Below Sign Out→Delete Account
1

Start in Profile
Use the profile tab in the app.

Step by step

It only takes a few taps.

Make sure you are signed in to the account you want to remove.

  1. 01

    Open Budget Gadget

    Launch the app and sign in to the account you want to delete.

  2. 02

    Tap Profile

    Use the Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar.

  3. 03

    Open About

    On the Profile screen, scroll to the relevant settings area and tap About.

  4. 04

    Scroll below Sign Out

    In About, scroll down past the Sign Out button. The Delete Account option appears underneath it.

  5. 05

    Tap Delete Account and confirm

    Read the confirmation message carefully, then confirm permanent deletion. This action cannot be undone.

Before you confirm

Know what deletion means.

Deleting an account is different from signing out or removing the app from your phone.

×

Account access ends

Your profile and access to account-only features are permanently removed.

⌁

Personal data is removed

Associated personal information is deleted or de-identified, subject to necessary legal and security retention.

!

Check active requests

Complete or resolve open orders, sell requests, repairs, refunds or payments before deletion.
