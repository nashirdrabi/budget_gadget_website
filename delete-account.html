Account & Data

Delete your account,
on your terms.

You're always in control of your data. Deleting your Budget Gadget account takes under a minute, right from the app's Account screen — no calls, no forms, no waiting.

1
Open the Budget Gadget app
Launch the app on your phone and log in to the account you want to delete.
2
Go to the Account screen
Tap the Account (profile) tab in the bottom navigation bar.
3
Tap "Delete Account"
Scroll to the bottom of the Account screen and tap Delete Account.
4
Confirm — and you're done
Confirm the deletion in the pop-up. Your account and personal data are permanently removed. This cannot be undone.

Good to Know

What happens next?

🧹
Your data is erased
Your profile, phone number, email, saved addresses, and payment details are permanently deleted from our systems.
🧾
Legal records only
Completed transaction records (invoices, device IMEIs) are kept only as long as Indian tax and anti-fraud law requires, then erased. See our Privacy Policy.
⏳
Open orders finish first
If you have a pickup or delivery in progress, complete or cancel it before deleting — active orders block deletion to protect your payout.
