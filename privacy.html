Privacy Policy

Your trust matters more to us than any transaction. This policy explains — in plain language — what data the Budget Gadget app collects, why we collect it, and how you stay in control of it.

🗓 Last updated: July 2, 2026

On this page

📋

1. Information We Collect

When you use the Budget Gadget app to buy, sell, or exchange smartphones, iPads, tablets, laptops, MacBooks, EarPods, earbuds, smartwatches, or any other gadget, we collect only what we need to serve you:

  • Account information — your name, phone number, and email address when you create an account.
  • Device & listing details — the make, model, condition, and IMEI/serial number of gadgets you sell or exchange, so we can verify them and quote a fair price.
  • Pickup address & location — the address you provide (and, with your permission, GPS location) so our agents can reach you for doorstep pickup and delivery.
  • Payment details — your UPI ID or bank account details, used solely to pay you instantly when we collect your device.
  • Usage & diagnostics — anonymous app analytics and crash reports that help us fix bugs and improve the experience.
⚙️

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate instant quotes for your phones, iPads, laptops, EarPods, and other gadgets.
  • To schedule and complete doorstep pickups and deliveries across the Kashmir valley.
  • To make instant UPI / bank payments when your device is collected.
  • To provide customer support and honour our 7-day warranty.
  • To prevent fraud — including IMEI verification to keep stolen devices off our platform.
  • To send order updates and offers (you can opt out of promotional messages at any time).

We never sell your personal data to anyone. Full stop.

🤝

3. Sharing & Disclosure

We share data only with the small set of partners needed to run the service:

  • Pickup agents — your name, address, and order details, only for the pickup or delivery assigned to them.
  • Payment providers — UPI and banking partners that process your payout.
  • Analytics & crash-reporting services — anonymised usage data only.
  • Law enforcement — if required by law, for example to trace a reported-stolen device.
🔒

4. Data Security

All data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections and stored on secured servers. Access to personal data is restricted to team members who need it to serve you, and payment credentials are handled by certified payment providers — we never store your full bank details on our own servers.

🗄️

5. Data Retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. Transaction records (invoices, device IMEIs) are retained as required by Indian law for tax and anti-fraud compliance, even after account deletion. Everything else is removed when you delete your account.

🧭

6. Your Rights

  • Access — ask us what personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction — update your name, address, or payment details anytime from the Account screen.
  • Deletion — permanently delete your account and associated data (see below).
  • Opt-out — disable promotional notifications from your device settings or the app.
🗑️

7. Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your Budget Gadget account directly inside the app — no phone calls, no emails required:

1
Open the Budget Gadget app
Log in if you aren't already.
2
Go to the Account screen
Tap the Account / Profile tab in the bottom navigation.
3
Tap "Delete Account"
You'll find it at the bottom of the Account screen.
4
Confirm deletion
Your account and personal data are permanently removed. This cannot be undone.

Deleting your account removes your profile, saved addresses, and payment details. Completed transaction records are retained only as long as Indian law requires, then erased.

8. Children's Privacy

Budget Gadget is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

🔄

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the service evolves. Significant changes will be announced in the app, and the "Last updated" date at the top of this page always reflects the current version.

💬
